In the Google Keep app on your Android device, you can now sort your notes in the primary browse view by “date created” and “date modified.” Visit the Help Center to learn more about labeling, coloring or pinning notes . Rollout details: Rapid Release and Scheduled Release domains : Available now. Availability: Available to all Google Workspace customers, Workspace Individual Subscribers, and users with personal Google accounts.
